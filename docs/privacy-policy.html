Privacy Policy — Pocket Bible

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Pocket Bible is an offline Bible-reading and devotional app. This policy covers the whole app, because there isn't much to cover: the app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or anyone else.

What the app does not do

  • It does not request internet access — the app has no INTERNET permission at all, so it cannot send data anywhere even if it wanted to.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or any third-party tracking SDK.
  • It does not require an account, sign-in, email address, or any other identifying information.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or any other device data or permission.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves a few things so it works the way you'd expect between sessions — your saved verses, any Bible reading bookmarks you set, and your chosen display language. All of this is stored in a local database on your device only, using Android's standard app storage. It is never sent off your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Links you can choose to open

The app's "About & Sources" screen links out to the public source pages and license text for the Bible translations it bundles (e.g. eBible.org, Creative Commons). Tapping one of these opens your device's own browser to an external site not operated by us — the privacy practices of those sites are their own, not covered by this policy.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data of any kind from anyone, this applies equally regardless of the user's age.

Changes to this policy

If this app ever starts collecting data in the future, this page will be updated first, before that change ships.
